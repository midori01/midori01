Most of my code happens in quiet corners—I've always preferred curling up on a couch or bed with my phone, rather than sitting anchored to a cold, heavy desk setup. Usually with quiet music playing, a warm black coffee (or an iced Americano on sunny afternoons) nearby, watching terminal lines quietly scroll by.

Almost everything here was put together inside Termux on Android—simply because staying cozy under a blanket will always beat sitting in front of a monitor.

I’ve never cared much for grand architecture debates. To me, strange build errors and panics are just quiet little puzzles—sitting down with AI, patiently coaxing each quirk into place until things finally click. I simply find peace in things being untangled, patches fitting with zero offset, and a clean, linear git trail.
